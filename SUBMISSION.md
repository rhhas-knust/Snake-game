## Title
Snake Pro: Ghana Edition

## Tagline (short description, one line)
A modern snake game with power-ups, combos, a Daily Challenge, and a secret level hidden inside.

## Description

> **Snake Pro: Ghana Edition** takes the classic snake game and gives it a Ghanaian identity. You eat pineapple, plantain, groundnuts and mango, dodge raw pepper, chase the ⭐ Black Star, count down in Twi (*Mmiɛnsa, Mmienu, Baako, KƆ!*), earn an *Ayekoo!* every level, and play to an original highlife-style soundtrack built on the West African bell pattern. Kente and Black Stars skins included.
>
> 🎮 **4 modes**:
> - **Classic:** walls are deadly.
> - **Portal:** the edges wrap around.
> - **Maze:** rocks grow every level.
> - **Zen:** no pressure.
>
> ⚡ **Power-ups:** 🛡️ Shield, ⏳ Slow-mo, 💎 Double points, 🧲 Magnet, ✂️ Shrink.
> 🔥 **Combos:** chain fruit quickly for up to x8 points.
> 📱 **Tilt steering:** steer by tilting your phone, with smoothing and a dead zone so it stays steady. Swipe or arrow buttons work too.
> ☠️ **Poison:** dodge coconuts and eggs. They vanish after a few seconds.
> 📅 **Daily Challenge:** everyone in the world gets the same board each day. Compare scores with friends.
> 📤 **Share card:** one tap turns your run into an image for TikTok, Instagram or WhatsApp.
> 🏆 **15 achievements**, two of them hidden.
> 🌀 **And a secret level…** Can you find it?
>
> Plays in any browser on phone or computer, installs as an app, and works offline. No ads, no accounts, no data collected.

## How it was made (devlog / "about" section)

> I started with a simple snake game that had real problems. Poison could make it unwinnable, and quick key presses were ignored. I rebuilt it step by step:
> 1. Game modes, power-ups and combos.
> 2. A secret level.
> 3. A Daily Challenge and shareable score cards, which my TikTok followers asked for.
> 4. A phone-first layout and an installable offline app.
>
> Before submitting, I had a bot play every mode to audit the game. That audit found and fixed four issues:
> - The secret level was giving out far too many points.
> - The Daily Challenge boards drifted apart between players.
> - Long snakes made slow phones stutter.
> - There were accessibility gaps. The game now supports screen readers and the "reduce motion" setting.
>
> Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no engine or libraries) and hosted on GitHub Pages. Follow the journey on TikTok: **@roberthasford**.
