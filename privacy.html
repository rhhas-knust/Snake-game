🐍 Snake Pro: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Short version: Snake Pro does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your own device.

What the game stores

To remember your progress, the game saves the following in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • Your best scores, Daily Challenge result and achievements
  • Your settings (mode, difficulty, skin, sound, arrow buttons)
  • Simple totals such as games played and fruits eaten

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data for this game in your browser or uninstalling the app.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names or email addresses
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access
  • No data sent to any server. The game's security policy blocks it from contacting other websites.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share, the game creates a score image on your device and hands it to your phone's or computer's own share menu. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose an app to share it with, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Offline use

If you install the app, its files are saved on your device so it can run without an internet connection.

Children

Snake Pro is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Hosting

The game's website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
