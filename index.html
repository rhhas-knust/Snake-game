How to play
  • Move with ←↑→↓ or WASD. Pause with P, Space or Esc. M mutes.
  • Swipe anywhere on the board, or use the arrow pad.
  • Fruit gives points and energy. Energy drains over time, so keep eating.
  • Eat fruit quickly one after another to build a combo (up to x8 points).
  • Every 5 fruits you level up and the snake gets faster.
  • 🥥 and 🥚 are poisonous. They vanish after a few seconds, so you can wait them out.
  • Power-ups: 🛡️ blocks one fatal hit, ⏳ slows time, 💎 doubles points, 🧲 pulls fruit toward you, ✂️ trims your tail.
  • Grab the rare 🌟 golden star before it disappears for a big bonus.
